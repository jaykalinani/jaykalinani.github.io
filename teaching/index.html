teaching & mentoring

Selected teaching, mentoring, and scientific community work.

Teaching

  • Guest lectures on Drag Forces and Harmonic Oscillators for undergraduate Classical Mechanics at UIUC.
  • Guest lectures on General Relativistic Magnetohydrodynamics and Numerical Methods for Conservation Laws for graduate courses at Rochester Institute of Technology.
  • Designed and presented tutorials on CarpetX and AsterX at Einstein Toolkit workshops and schools.

Mentoring

  • Co-mentor to graduate researchers Allen Wen, Amara Nusbickel, Maxwell Rizzo, Noora Ghadiri, and Freddy Pardoe.
  • Hackathon mentor to Bing-Jyun Tsao and Maxwell Rizzo.

Scientific leadership

  • Local and scientific organizer, 2026 North American Einstein Toolkit Workshop and School at UIUC.
  • Local organizer, 2025 CSSI Einstein Toolkit Hackathon.
  • Team lead, AsterX/CarpetX group at the 2023 Frontier Hackathon.
  • Organizer of CCRG research seminars.

Outreach

Since 2020, I have volunteered with Skype A Scientist, leading astronomy sessions for classrooms and families. I have also contributed to Imagine RIT, organized public “Let’s Talk Astronomy” sessions, and written for Astrobites.
