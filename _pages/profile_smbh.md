title: SMBH binary accretion

Simulations of gas dynamics and accretion in **merging supermassive black hole binaries**, tracking minidisks, streams, and post-merger behavior, with the goal of predicting electromagnetic counterparts across bands.

- Related repos: <https://github.com/lwJi/SpacetimeX>
- Focus: EM signatures during inspiral–merger–ringdown
