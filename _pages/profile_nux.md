
---
title: nuX
---
**nuX** is a moment-based (M1) neutrino-transport module designed for GPU hardware. It aims to couple tightly with AsterX to enable multimessenger GRMHD simulations with realistic neutrino physics.

- Repo: <https://github.com/jaykalinani/nuX>
- Focus: Neutrino radiation transport, BNS post-merger & collapsar contexts
